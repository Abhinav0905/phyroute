## Contribution boundary

Do not claim that previous power-flow research ignores when to invoke an exact solver. Shamseldein and Nawaz directly preclude that claim. Do not claim that the benchmark proves neural uncertainty intrinsically fails while physics always succeeds. The stale ensemble receives operating features without current topology; the verifier and residual model receive current-physics information. Paired operating points intentionally expose that information difference.

The defensible contribution is a controlled test of when a neural surrogate adds value beyond LinDistFlow plus exact fallback, with explicit topology allocation, independent threshold calibration, matched budgets, verifier mismatch and unresolved-reference accounting. Report any gains alongside false rejections, realized escalation and complete-route time. The held-out set concerns untrained branch exchanges on one feeder, not unseen distribution networks. A negative or mixed result can support this contribution.

## Other source checks used in the audit

- A DC power-flow solver is unsuitable as the independent verifier of a voltage-magnitude band: its approximation fixes magnitudes near 1 pu. Use AC equations or a declared radial voltage approximation.
